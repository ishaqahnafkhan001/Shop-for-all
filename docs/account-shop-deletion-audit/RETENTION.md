# Active obligations, shared data and retention

This is a technical decision matrix, not legal advice or an approved retention schedule. **No Bangladesh tax/privacy/consumer-law retention period is asserted.** Application fields and TTLs do not establish legal authority to destroy or retain a record.

## Proposed eligibility principle

An owner may request closure, but irreversible purge must wait until obligations and holds are classified. The simplest first implementation should refuse purge while unresolved commerce/financial obligations exist and provide actionable reasons. Product/operations must separately decide whether the request immediately closes new sales while obligations are resolved. If closure is immediate, a restricted fulfillment/refund/customer-history capability must exist; ordinary storefront availability cannot provide it today.

No indefinite blanket retention. Each retained category needs a documented purpose, lawful/business basis, minimum fields, responsible role, retention trigger, finite expiry or review date, access boundary, deletion method and evidence. A legal hold requires a recorded reason and periodic review; a missing policy is a blocker, not a permanent hold by default.

## Resource treatment categories

| Category | Candidate action after approved eligibility | Minimum retained information / isolation |
|---|---|---|
| Catalog, variants, categories, collections, banners, promotions, scheduled sales, drafts/revisions, branding/SEO/domain config | Delete tenant records and eligible owned assets after manifest capture. | Exclude shared theme code, vendor plans, other shops' references and assets needed for retained evidence. |
| Abandoned carts, tracking events, product/shop metrics, AI content/results/history/drafts and marketing campaign content | Delete or irreversibly aggregate approved statistics. | Hashing an identifier alone is pseudonymization; do not label it anonymization. Audit raw payloads and embedded media URLs. |
| Orders, returns/refunds, purchase orders, invoices, payments, inventory/audit evidence | Retain temporarily or anonymize under a category-specific schedule; delete unnecessary notes, contact details and proof when their purpose ends. | Immutable commercial snapshots may remain necessary. Existing renderers populate Shop/User/Product; compatibility must be tested before deleting parent records. |
| Consent and suppression evidence | Stop all marketing; retain only what is justified to prove consent/withdrawal or prevent prohibited re-contact. | Determine whether a tenant disappears entirely or suppression must survive an email re-import. Do not delete a shared person's global preferences blindly. |
| CustomerProfile, tenant User and membership | Remove shop relationship after obligations are transferred to approved minimal historical access. | Preserve global Account and other shops' profiles/memberships. Customer credentials are not the deleting vendor's property. |
| Vendor/staff User, permissions and membership | Revoke scoped sessions immediately, delete tenant artifacts late. | Staff global Accounts remain independent even if they have no other memberships. Owner Account deletion requires separate scope eligibility. |
| Owner Account and platform/support roles | Delete only with explicit account-scope authorization and no unresolved other relationships; otherwise retain account access elsewhere. | Cross-account notes/audit fields can be scrubbed independently without removing the shared Account. |
| Verification NID and business documents | Revoke document access at shutdown; remove when legally/business eligible. | Raw NID, signed URLs, migrated originals and uploaded replacement versions require distinct manifest entries; existing retention fields are not an enforced universal purge policy. |
| Operational logs and minimal deletion ledger | Minimize; enforce approved finite raw log retention. Keep a minimal suppression record as long as restoration risk requires. | Completion evidence must not retain whole shop snapshots, customer data, credentials, NID or unnecessary original media URLs. |
| Backups, exported data, recipient inboxes, shipping/payment provider records | Expire or submit supported erasure requests according to actual contracts; state limitations explicitly. | No claim of immediate physical destruction from application database deletion. |

## Existing technical retention is not a legal schedule

Confirmed: raw analytics default TTL is 180 days; AI product history 30 days and campaign drafts 90 days; settled AI operation TTL is set only after accounting recovery/receipt pruning; unresolved operations can have no TTL. Builder temporary assets have 24-hour eligibility and retired assets a seven-day grace, extended when referenced. AuditLog cleanup uses subscription activity-log limits and explicitly preserves orphaned logs when plan access cannot resolve. Password reset/OTP and quota reservations have operational TTLs. These mechanisms do not erase all tenant data on demand. ## Bangladesh questions for current qualified legal verification

Obtain current advice identifying applicable instruments and exact clauses, entity type, place of business and effective dates. Do not copy an assumed generic number of years into code.

- Which VAT/tax/accounting invoice, payment, inventory and purchase records must this platform and the merchant each keep, and from what trigger?
- Which consumer protection, return/refund, warranty, delivery and dispute obligations survive shop closure, and who must provide access?
- What lawful basis and limits apply to NID/business-document storage, verification records, IP/user-agent logs, consent proof and fraud/abuse evidence?
- Which data protection/deletion, consent, cross-border processor, notification and backup-expiry requirements currently apply to this Bangladesh service and its customers?
- Are electronic invoices/snapshots sufficient; what fields and integrity evidence are required; can contact/NID data be removed independently?
- How are legal holds authorized/released, and how must immutable/provider backups and foreign processors be handled?
- Can a minimal deletion/anti-resurrection ledger be retained, with which identifiers and review period?

Record answers as a versioned retention policy. They are release blockers for destructive categories; the audit does not resolve them.
