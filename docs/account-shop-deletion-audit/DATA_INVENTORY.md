## Reading the matrix

- `S`: authenticated, proven target Shop ID; `A`: explicitly authorized owner Account ID. `U/M/P/O/T(S)`: User/membership/Product/Order/SupportTicket IDs whose shop ownership is captured before parent deletion. Lists must be bounded/keyset-paged and persisted where needed.
- **Confirmed:** model fields, compiled collection names and linked consumer imports. All actions below are **proposed**, subject to approved retention. Consumer links locate real controllers/services; they are not claims that those modules already perform deletion.
- Direct selectors always retain their tenant predicate. For legacy records missing a tenant key or records with contradictory shop/foreign-ID ownership, flag and reconcile them. Do not widen a delete to a cross-tenant `OR` query or infer ownership from matching email, phone, URL hostname, actor ID or folder prefix.
- R0: content/operational data, normally purge after approved eligibility. R1: commercial/financial/customer obligation. R2: independent/shared identity. R3: consent, privacy, fraud, verification or support evidence. R4: shared platform record, preserve or narrowly scrub. R5: processing/idempotency/uncertain external outcome, settle or quarantine before purge. These are classifications, **not legal retention durations**; see RETENTION.md.
- V1: requery eligible rows with exact typed selectors, including soft-deleted/archived rows, and compare retained allowlist/counts. V2: independent foreign-reference/manifest residual sweep after parent removal; unknown conflicts fail verification. V3: provider absence/revocation/invalidation outcome for every eligible item; declared external holds remain visible. V4: unrelated tenant/account/shared-resource fingerprints and functional checks remain unchanged. Scope hashes must not expose customer data.
